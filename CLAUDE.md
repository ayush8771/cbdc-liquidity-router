# CBDC Liquidity Router — Claude Code Project Instructions

## Project objective
Build and evaluate a reinforcement-learning-based liquidity routing prototype for cross-border CBDC transactions. The routing policy allocates each transaction across feasible bridge venues while accounting for available liquidity, congestion, cost, and risk. Stage 1D is implemented and experimentally evaluated (see `docs/STAGE1D_RESULTS.md`). Stage 1E (Cognee / semantic memory) is complete as an infrastructure milestone; populating it with real regulatory knowledge is deferred (see `docs/STAGE1E_MEMORY.md`). Stage 1F (comprehensive evaluation) is the next planned milestone and has not been started.

## Current research status
Stage 1D experiments are finalized. `docs/STAGE1D_RESULTS.md` has the numbers and output folders; it is the authoritative summary.

- **Entropy:** the paired multi-seed comparison (seeds 42, 123 and 2026, validation) showed no consistent benefit from 0.005. The mean reward difference was −0.0007 ± 0.0014. The default is **0.0**.
- **Failures:** every diagnosed PPO failure was a `splitting_needed` transaction in GBR→PHL, caused by over-allocating one bridge beyond its liquidity with no redistribution.
- **Capacity projection:** inference-time projection raises PPO completion to the waterfall's level on every checkpoint, on validation and on all holdouts (+3.6 to +6.8 points on the holdouts). It involves no retraining, and it is a correction applied after the policy, not learned behaviour.
- **PPO vs waterfall on reward is inconclusive.** Projected PPO minus waterfall ranges from −0.0033 to +0.0051 across the five holdout views, and projected PPO wins on 2 to 6 of the 6 checkpoints. Do not claim PPO beats the waterfall.
- **No independent out-of-time holdout exists.** The holdouts share the test period, and most rows' 30-day history windows overlap already-examined dates.
- **Remaining Stage 1F work:**
  - an independent out-of-time holdout, which requires extending the data past 2025-09-30;
  - calibration of the reward weights (all λ are 1.0 placeholders), and sensitivity analysis;
  - confidence intervals for the PPO-vs-waterfall comparison;
  - better coverage of `exceeds_combined`;
  - deciding whether capacity awareness should be learned rather than projected.

None of this has been started without approval.

**Stage 1E** is complete as an infrastructure milestone; `docs/STAGE1E_MEMORY.md` is authoritative.
- **Feasibility comes only from the deterministic knowledge base**, through `StaticMemoryProvider`. Cognee is limited to explanations and audit, and knowledge is never a PPO input.
- **Never invent facts.** New facts need a real, cited source. `verified: true` is only for checked `real_*` sources.
- **Approval needed:** letting dated or corridor-scoped facts affect feasibility requires an approved environment change.
- **Keep the cap in sync:** the generator's $500K cap must equal the knowledge-base cap, which a test enforces.

- **Storage:** `.cognee/` is git-ignored and rebuildable.
