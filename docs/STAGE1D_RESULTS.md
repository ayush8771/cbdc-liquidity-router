# Stage 1D — Results Summary

**Status:** Stage 1D implementation is complete, and experimental validation is complete for the questions below.
**Scope:** simulation-based findings under stated assumptions. They are not claims about real CBDC infrastructure.
**Source of truth:** the output folders cited in each section. Every number here is copied from those folders'
JSON/CSV files. If this summary and a folder disagree, the folder wins.

---

## 1. Setup (common to all experiments)

- **Network:** 3 corridors (USA→IND, GBR→PHL, ARE→MEX), with 3 venues each (9 in total). The 3 original venues are tagged
  `real_static_bridge`; the 6 additional venues are tagged `synthetic_bridge_expansion`.
- **Policy:** a PPO actor-critic. It encodes each feasible bridge's 30-day liquidity/congestion window with a shared Stage 1C
  1D CNN, and combines those with 31 raw features (415 dimensions in total). The action head is a masked Dirichlet over feasible slots. Each transaction is
  a one-step episode (`GAMMA = 0`). The deterministic action is the normalized Dirichlet concentration.
- **Execution:** `executed_i = min(requested_i, liquidity_i)`. Clipped amounts are **not** redistributed.
- **Reward:** `cost_term − λL·latency − λR·risk − λU·unrouted`, with **all λ = 1.0 placeholders (not calibrated)**.
- **Baselines:**
  - *cheapest-feasible*: 100% of the amount on the lowest-fee bridge;
  - *liquidity-aware waterfall*: fill bridges in fee order, each up to its current liquidity.
- **Data:** synthetic transactions, stratified by regime, are generated by `src/training/transaction_generator.py`. The
  train/validation/test splits cover 2011–2021, 2022–2023 and 2024–2025-09-30 respectively, with 30-day gap buffers. The ground-truth regime
  labels are `below_cheapest`, `near_boundary`, `splitting_needed` and `exceeds_combined`.
- **Training:** 20 epochs, batch size 32, learning rate 3e-4, 4 PPO epochs per update. Each run keeps the checkpoint with the best
  validation reward.

## 2. Entropy-regularisation experiments

### 2a. Seed 42, test split (`outputs/experiments/stage1d_entropy_pair_seed42_20261031_153140/`)

| PPO metric (test, n=100) | `ENTROPY_COEF` 0.0 | `ENTROPY_COEF` 0.005 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mean reward | 0.888615 | 0.899642 |
| Completion rate | 0.86 | 0.94 |
| `splitting_needed` completion | 70.83% | 87.5% |
| Best validation reward (epoch) | 0.896450 (4) | 0.895414 (17) |

On the same split, the waterfall scored a mean reward of 0.919709 with 100% completion. This was a single seed on a test
split that had already been examined.

### 2b. Paired multi-seed, validation split (`outputs/experiments/stage1d_entropy_multiseed_20261101_180551/`)

Seeds 42, 123 and 2026 × coefficients {0.0, 0.005}. These runs were compared on the validation split only.

| PPO metric (validation, mean ± SD over 3 seeds) | 0.0 | 0.005 | Δ (0.005 − 0.0) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mean reward | 0.8989 ± 0.0037 | 0.8982 ± 0.0027 | −0.0007 ± 0.0014 |
| Completion rate | 0.950 ± 0.030 | 0.947 ± 0.023 | −0.003 ± 0.015 |

**Finding:** there is no consistent benefit from 0.005, and the sign of the difference varies by seed. The default
`ENTROPY_COEF` is **0.0**; 0.005 is kept as `EXPERIMENTAL_ENTROPY_COEF`. The seed-42 reruns did not reproduce the
original seed-42 validation rewards bit-for-bit (0.895447 vs 0.896450).

## 3. Failure diagnosis (`outputs/diagnostics/failed_splitting_20261101_202140/`, authoritative)

All 8 checkpoints were replayed: the 2 seed-42 test checkpoints and the 6 multi-seed checkpoints on validation. Each
replay reproduced its saved metrics exactly.

- There were 51 failed (checkpoint, transaction) rows. **All** of them are `splitting_needed` transactions in the **GBR→PHL** corridor.
- **The mechanism in 51 of 51 cases:** over-allocation. At least one bridge was asked for more than its liquidity, and the excess was clipped and
  not redistributed. No failure exceeded combined liquidity; the highest ratio of amount to combined liquidity was 0.9395.
- The clipped excess had a median of 5.13% of the amount, with a range of 0.18–23.78%.
- **Stochastic samples:** 200 samples were drawn per transaction. In 40 of 51 rows, at least one single sample both completes the transaction and beats the
  deterministic reward.
- **Unrouted-amount accounting:** the environment counts unrouted as *requested − executed*. An all-zero action
  therefore scores 0.0, and that is ≥ the waterfall's reward in 37 of 51 of these high-congestion rows. PPO's actions always sum
  to 1, so PPO cannot exploit this. It is a point about how the reward and metrics are defined.

## 4. Capacity projection (inference-time; `outputs/experiments/stage1d_capacity_projection_20261101_210614/`, authoritative)

`src/training/capacity_projection.py` applies a Euclidean projection to the deterministic PPO action. The target set is
`{0 ≤ x_i ≤ liquidity_i/amount, Σx = 1}`. If combined liquidity is below the amount, the projection fills every cap instead.

- This is an evaluation-time wrapper. It involves **no retraining and no change to the model, environment or reward**.
- The liquidity values it uses are already part of PPO's observation.
- 20 unit tests cover it, including checks of the exact optimality (KKT) conditions, an independent brute-force grid search, and the handling of NaN and infinite inputs.

Validation results, mean over 6 checkpoints. These are optimistic, because validation also selected each checkpoint's best epoch:

| Metric | Original PPO | Projected PPO | Waterfall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Completion | 0.948 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| Mean reward | 0.89853 | 0.89893 | 0.88009 |

The projection changes only the transactions that original PPO had failed, and it completes all of them.

All of these were generated by the unchanged generator using new seeds, in the generator's last ("test") date window. The results
below are means over the 6 checkpoints.

| …without corridor-dates shared with the test split | `stage1d_holdout_sensitivity_20261101_230534` | 470 | 227/145/95/3 | +6.8 pts | −0.0033 | 2/6 |

On every holdout, projected PPO and the waterfall have the same amount-based completion. That is 100%, except on rows where combined liquidity is
insufficient, which neither policy can complete.

**Unrouted accounting for `exceeds_combined` rows:** for these rows, the environment-reported completion overstates the
waterfall and projected PPO. Both request less than the full amount, and the part that is never requested is not counted as unrouted. The holdout scripts therefore report an
amount-based figure as well (`amount − executed`).

## 6. Key findings

1. **Capacity-aware execution matters.** Every PPO failure examined is a small over-allocation on one liquidity-limited
   bridge, with spare liquidity left on another.
2. **Inference-time projection closes the completion gap.** It does so on every checkpoint and on every holdout, without retraining.
3. **Reward superiority over the waterfall is inconclusive.** Projected PPO minus the waterfall ranges from −0.0033 to +0.0051 across the
   holdout views, and it beats the waterfall on between 2 and 6 of the 6 checkpoints depending on which holdout is drawn. The regime-level pattern also flips
   between holdouts. On validation, PPO's apparent advantage (0.899 vs 0.880) is affected by checkpoint selection.
4. **Entropy regularisation (0.005) shows no consistent benefit** across seeds on validation or on the holdouts.

## 7. Limitations

- **No independent out-of-time holdout was possible.** The daily liquidity/congestion data, the macro-risk data and the RPW cost data all end in
  2025-09, the same date as the end of the test window. Every holdout therefore shares the test period with the already-examined test split. Even
  after excluding dates, most rows' 30-day history windows overlap the history of an examined date in the same corridor. The mean overlap ranges from 20.6 to 28.0 of 30 days, depending on the reference set:

  | Holdout view | Reference set | Rows with overlap |
  |---|---|---|
  | Seed 20261102, without any date shared with the test split | test split | 393 of 422 |
  | Seed 20261103 | test split | 473 of 500 |
  | Seed 20261104 | test split and seed-20261102 holdout combined | 500 of 500 |

  A genuinely independent holdout would require extending the data past 2025-09-30.
- **Placeholder reward weights:** λ = 1.0 for every term. All reward comparisons depend on these uncalibrated weights.
- **Synthetic dynamics:** liquidity and congestion are synthetic. Transactions are synthetic and stratified by regime.
- **Small scale:** 3 corridors and 6 checkpoints from 3 seeds; failures are concentrated in a single corridor (GBR→PHL).
- **`exceeds_combined` is too rare to evaluate.** There are 0–3 rows per holdout, because of the $500K regulatory cap.
- **Not a learned behaviour:** the projection is a correction applied after the policy. It does not make PPO itself capacity-aware.
- **Reproducibility:** the seed-42 multi-seed retraining did not reproduce the original seed-42 run bit-for-bit. Checkpoint replays and
  evaluations are exactly reproducible on the recorded hardware (CUDA, RTX 3050).
